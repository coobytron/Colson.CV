A walk through the grove.

Each glowing mushroom is a chapter. The Gardener walks you through them in order, or you can wander on your own.

  1. Move WASD or the arrows. In Free explore, tap the ground too.
  2. Inspect Tap a glowing mushroom or its label to read about it.
  3. Open a demo Go to Experiments, pick a project, press Open demo.

· press ? for all controls

How to play

Tour

  • N / PageDown next stop · B / PageUp previous
  • 1–6 jump to a stop
  • F free explore · G back to the tour
  • L jump to the Local Lab (from anywhere)
  • R back to the tour from Free Explore (to the stop you left)
  • P presentation mode: just ← → (or a clicker) through every section
  • The tour never moves on by itself

Presenter view

  • V labels on / off · I card on / off
  • + − text size (remembered)
  • H hide everything · Full fullscreen
  • Top right: the same as buttons

Gardener & camera

  • WASD / arrows walk · Shift run · C sit
  • Drag look around · Scroll / pinch zoom
  • Tap a mushroom or its label to visit it
  • H hide the interface · ? this help
